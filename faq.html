Jewellery

ALANKRA pieces are designed with anti-tarnish qualities.

However, anti-tarnish does not mean tarnish-proof. Exposure to moisture, perfume, lotions, chemicals and other environmental factors can affect jewellery over time.

Following our Jewellery Care guide can help maintain the finish of your pieces.
Our jewellery is described as water-resistant, not completely waterproof.

We recommend removing your jewellery before showering, swimming, exercising or exposing it to prolonged moisture.
Our pieces are designed with everyday wear in mind.

For the best longevity, we recommend following the Jewellery Care guide and avoiding unnecessary exposure to water, perfume, lotions and harsh chemicals.
Keep your jewellery dry, wipe it gently with a soft dry cloth after wear and store each piece separately when possible.

Avoid perfume, lotions, creams, sanitiser, harsh chemicals, swimming and prolonged exposure to moisture.

For the complete guide, visit Jewellery Care .
