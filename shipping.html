Good To Know

Shipping Details

Processing

Orders are prepared for dispatch after they are placed. The exact processing timeline may vary depending on order volume and product availability.

Your dispatch confirmation will provide the relevant shipment information.

Delivery

Delivery timelines can vary depending on the destination, courier network, weekends, holidays and circumstances outside our control.

Please allow the expected delivery window shown for your order.

Shipping Charges

Applicable shipping charges, if any, will be communicated clearly during the ordering process before you complete your purchase.

No unexpected shipping charge should appear after your order is confirmed.

Order Tracking

Once your order has been dispatched, tracking information may be provided where available so you can follow its journey.

Please keep your tracking details handy until your order has arrived safely.

Please Note

Delivery addresses matter.

Please carefully check your name, phone number, complete address, PIN code and other delivery details before placing your order. Incorrect or incomplete information may cause delivery delays or failed delivery attempts.

When It Arrives

A little care
at your doorstep.

When your package arrives, we recommend checking the outer packaging before opening. If anything appears damaged, keep the packaging and contact us as soon as possible.

Your order should reach you safely, beautifully packed and ready to wear.

Still Have Questions?

We're here to help.

For questions about your order, delivery, returns or anything else, please reach out to ALANKRA.
