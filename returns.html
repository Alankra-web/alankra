Good To Know

Before Requesting a Return

Return Window

The applicable return or exchange window will depend on the final ALANKRA policy associated with your order.

Please contact us promptly if you believe your order requires a return or exchange.

Original Condition

Items considered for return or exchange should generally be unused, unworn and in their original condition, packaging and presentation unless the issue relates to damage or an incorrect item.

Proof of Purchase

Please keep your order confirmation, order number or other purchase details available when contacting ALANKRA.

These details help us locate your order quickly.

Review First

A return or exchange request is not automatically approved. Each request may need to be reviewed based on the condition of the item and the reason for the request.

Keep In Mind

What Usually Helps

For a Smoother Request

  • Keep the original jewellery packaging.
  • Keep your order confirmation or order number.
  • Contact us promptly when an issue is noticed.
  • Keep the item unused and in its original condition when requesting a change for reasons other than damage.
  • Share clear photographs when requested.

Please Avoid

  • Sending an item back without contacting ALANKRA first.
  • Removing or discarding original packaging before your request has been reviewed.
  • Wearing or altering an item before requesting an exchange for a change-of-mind reason.
  • Using abrasive cleaners or attempting repairs before contacting us about a product issue.
  • Discarding the product or packaging when reporting a damaged delivery.
Something Isn't Right?

We'll help you
figure it out.

If your order arrives damaged, incorrect or with another issue, please contact ALANKRA as soon as possible.

Keep the product, packaging and delivery materials until we've reviewed your request.

Clear photographs and your order details may help us understand the issue faster.

An Important Note

Please contact us before sending anything back.

Do not ship a return independently or to an address found elsewhere on the internet. Wait for ALANKRA to review your request and provide the appropriate instructions.
