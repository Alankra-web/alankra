Care, Made Simple

Everyday habits that help your ALANKRA jewellery stay beautiful for longer.

01
✦

Wear It

Your jewellery is made to be enjoyed. Wear your favourite pieces confidently as part of your everyday style.

02
◇

Store It

When you're not wearing your jewellery, keep each piece in a clean, dry place away from direct sunlight and unnecessary friction.

03

Gently wipe your jewellery with a soft, clean cloth after extended wear to remove everyday oils and residue.

Everyday Luxury

Designed for life. Loved for longer.

Water-resistant and anti-tarnish doesn't mean your jewellery needs no care. Simple habits can help maintain its beautiful finish and keep every detail looking its best. Because luxury isn't only about how something looks when you first receive it. It's about how much you continue to love it.

A Little Attention

The Do's & Don'ts

Do

  • ✦ Store your jewellery in a clean, dry and protected place.
  • ✦ Wipe pieces gently with a soft cloth after extended wear.
  • ✦ Keep delicate pieces separated when storing them.
  • ✦ Handle chains and delicate details gently when putting them on or off.

Don't

  • ◇ Use abrasive cleaners or rough materials on your jewellery.
  • ◇ Store jewellery loose with other pieces where it can rub against them.
  • ◇ Expose your jewellery unnecessarily to harsh chemicals or cleaning products.
  • ◇ Pull, twist or force delicate chains, clasps or charms.
