Jewellery Care

deserve beautiful care.

Your jewellery becomes part of your everyday moments. A few simple habits can help preserve its finish, shine and beauty for longer.

The Essentials

Simple Care Rituals

01
✦

Store With Care

Store each piece separately in a clean, dry pouch or jewellery box to reduce scratches, tangling and unnecessary exposure to humidity.

02
◇

Wear Mindfully

ALANKRA pieces are designed for everyday styling, but gentle handling helps preserve their finish and appearance.

03
♡

Keep Them Clean

After wearing, gently wipe your jewellery with a soft, clean cloth to remove everyday residue, oils and fingerprints.

04
✧

Avoid Chemicals

Keep jewellery away from perfumes, sprays, lotions, creams, sanitizers and harsh household chemicals whenever possible.

05
✦

Give It a Rest

When you're not wearing your favourite pieces, keep them safely stored instead of leaving them exposed on surfaces.

06
◇

Handle Gently

Put jewellery on after getting ready and remove it carefully to avoid unnecessary pulling, bending or impact.

A Little Reminder

Everyday luxury, thoughtfully cared for.

ALANKRA jewellery is designed to complement your everyday style. While our pieces are made with everyday wear in mind, proper care can help maintain their beautiful appearance for longer.

Your jewellery should fit into your life — a little care simply helps it stay beautiful.

Water & Moisture

A little extra care

Water-Resistant

ALANKRA jewellery is positioned as water-resistant for everyday use. However, water-resistant does not mean waterproof.

For best longevity, avoid prolonged or unnecessary exposure to water.

When To Remove It

We recommend removing your jewellery before showering, swimming, exercising or entering situations where it may be exposed to prolonged moisture.

If your jewellery becomes wet, gently dry it with a soft cloth before storing it.
Keep In Mind

The ALANKRA Guide

Do

  • Store your jewellery in its pouch or box.
  • Wipe pieces gently with a soft cloth.
  • Keep individual pieces separated when stored.
  • Put jewellery on after applying beauty products.
  • Remove jewellery before swimming or exercising.
  • Make sure pieces are dry before storing them.

Don't

  • Expose jewellery unnecessarily to perfumes or chemicals.
  • Wear jewellery for prolonged water exposure.
  • Pull, bend or force delicate jewellery.
  • Use abrasive cleaners or rough cloths.
  • Leave pieces scattered on hard surfaces.
  • Store jewellery while it is dirty or damp.
