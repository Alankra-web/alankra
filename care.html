Jewellery Care

Beautiful pieces
deserve beautiful care.

Your jewellery becomes part of your everyday moments. With a few simple habits, you can help preserve its finish, shine and beauty for longer.

The Essentials

Simple Care Rituals

01
✦

Store With Care

Store each piece separately in a clean, dry pouch or jewellery box to reduce scratches and tangling.

02
◇

Wear Mindfully

Although ALANKRA pieces are designed for everyday wear, treating them gently helps preserve their finish.

03
♡

Keep Them Clean

Gently wipe your jewellery with a soft clean cloth after wearing to remove everyday residue and fingerprints.

04
✧

Avoid Harsh Chemicals

Keep jewellery away from perfumes, sprays, creams, lotions and harsh household chemicals whenever possible.

05
✦

Give It a Rest

When you're not wearing your favourite pieces, store them safely rather than leaving them exposed on surfaces.

06
◇

Handle Gently

Put jewellery on after getting ready and remove it carefully to avoid unnecessary pulling, bending or impact.

A Little Reminder

Everyday luxury, thoughtfully cared for.

ALANKRA jewellery is designed to be part of your everyday style. While our pieces are made with durability in mind, proper care can help maintain their beautiful appearance for longer.

Your jewellery should fit into your life — and a little care keeps it looking its best.

Keep In Mind

The ALANKRA Guide

Do

  • Store your jewellery in its pouch or box.
  • Wipe pieces gently with a soft cloth.
  • Handle delicate chains and clasps carefully.
  • Keep individual pieces separated when stored.
  • Put jewellery on after applying beauty products.

Don't

  • Expose jewellery unnecessarily to perfumes or chemicals.
  • Pull, bend or force delicate jewellery.
  • Leave pieces scattered on hard surfaces.
  • Use abrasive cleaners or rough cloths.
  • Store jewellery while it is dirty or damp.
