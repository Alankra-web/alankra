ALANKRA privacy

Your information,
treated with care.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you visit or interact with the ALANKRA website.

Last updated: September 2026

1. About this policy

ALANKRA respects your privacy and aims to handle personal information responsibly. This policy describes the general types of information that may be collected through our website and how that information may be used.

By using the website, you acknowledge that information may be processed as described in this policy, subject to applicable law.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with the website, ALANKRA may receive information such as:

  • Your name and email address when you contact us.
  • Information you voluntarily include in a customer enquiry or message.
  • Order-related information when purchases are made through the website.
  • Delivery information required to fulfil an order, where applicable.
  • Information that may be generated through normal website use, such as browser or device information, depending on the technologies active on the website.

We do not ask you to provide sensitive personal information through ordinary website enquiries.

3. How we may use information

Information provided to ALANKRA may be used for purposes such as:

  • Responding to customer questions and enquiries.
  • Processing and managing orders, where applicable.
  • Communicating with you about an order or customer-service request.
  • Helping resolve shipping, return or exchange enquiries.
  • Maintaining and improving the website and customer experience.
  • Protecting the website and its users from misuse or fraudulent activity.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

4. Contact information

If you contact ALANKRA by email, the information contained in your message may be used to respond to your enquiry and provide customer support.

Official customer-care email: alankra.jewels@gmail.com

5. Orders and purchases

When online purchasing is active, information necessary to process and fulfil an order may be collected or shared with relevant service providers involved in completing that transaction.

Payment information should be handled through the payment provider presented during checkout. ALANKRA does not ask customers to send passwords, OTPs, PINs or banking credentials by email, Instagram direct message or other informal channels.

The specific payment provider and its own privacy practices will apply when you use a third-party payment service.

6. Cookies and browser storage

Websites may use technologies such as cookies or browser storage to remember certain information or support website functionality.

ALANKRA's website may use browser storage for functional features such as remembering shopping bag information on your device.

Browser settings may allow you to control or clear stored website information. Disabling certain browser technologies may affect some website functionality.

7. Third-party services

ALANKRA may use third-party services when needed to operate the website, process orders, facilitate payments, deliver products or provide other business functions.

Third-party services may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. Where relevant, customers should review those policies before using the applicable service.

ALANKRA does not control the privacy practices of independent third-party websites or services.

8. Sharing of information

ALANKRA does not intend to sell your personal information simply because you use our website.

Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to operate the business, fulfil an order, provide customer support, use an applicable third-party service, protect the website, or comply with a legal requirement.

9. Data security

ALANKRA takes reasonable steps to protect information under its control. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Customers should also take reasonable precautions, including keeping account credentials private and never sharing OTPs, passwords or payment PINs.

10. External links

The ALANKRA website may contain links to external websites or platforms, including social-media services.

Once you leave the ALANKRA website, the privacy practices and policies of the external service will apply. ALANKRA is not responsible for the privacy practices of independent third-party sites.

11. Your choices

You may contact ALANKRA if you have questions about information you have provided directly to us.

Depending on the circumstances and applicable law, you may also have rights concerning access, correction, deletion or other handling of your personal information.

Requests can be sent to: alankra.jewels@gmail.com

12. Children's privacy

The ALANKRA website is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that the matter can be reviewed.

13. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as ALANKRA's website, services or business practices develop.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.

14. Contact us

If you have a question about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact ALANKRA.

Email: alankra.jewels@gmail.com
